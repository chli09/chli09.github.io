Hi, I'm Chuanhan Li. I'm an MS student in Computer Science & Engineering at **UC Santa Cruz**, advised by [Prof. Yuanchao Xu](https://yuanchaoxu6.github.io/), and an incoming PhD student at **UC Riverside CSE** starting Fall 2026, co-advised by [Prof. Elaheh Sadredini](https://www.cs.ucr.edu/~elaheh/) and [Prof. Daniel Wong](https://www.danielwong.org/).

My research sits at the intersection of computer architecture, GPU systems, and hardware security. Recent and ongoing work:

- **GPU kernel optimization** — contributing to the open-source [cuLA](#) project on KDA linear-attention kernels for small batch/head regimes; current focus on CUTLASS-based tiling for autoregressive decoding on Hopper (H100).
- **GPU microarchitecture & security** — characterizing timing side channels arising from L2 cache eviction-priority hints on NVIDIA Ampere/Hopper.
- **Confidential computing for CXL memory** — first author of [AIORE (MICRO 2025)](assets/pdf/aiore.pdf), an adaptive incremental offloaded (re-)encryption design for CXL secure memory.

Before UCSC I received my MS from **Huazhong University of Science and Technology** (2021–2024) and BS from **Central South University** (2017–2021). I also spent a year at the HUST–Huawei Joint Innovation Lab.

Outside of research I spend my time on music (R&B, jazz, rock).
